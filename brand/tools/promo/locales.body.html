The same sentence, {{locale_count}} locales

Nobody agrees about quotation marks.

German lowers the opening quote. Swiss German turns it into guillemets pointing outward. French puts a no-break space inside them (U+00A0) and a narrow one before ! ? ; (U+202F). Finnish and Swedish close with the same glyph they open with. British English swaps the primary and secondary levels round. Every one of these is correct — in exactly one place.
